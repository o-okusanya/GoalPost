📊 Analytics Dashboard Guide

Overview

This analytics dashboard shows your personal goal-tracking performance pulled directly from your goals data.

🔢 Key Metrics

  • Goals Completed: Goals finished (checkbox ticked or progress at 100%).
  • On-Track Rate: % of progress goals at 50% or higher.
  • Avg. Completion: Average % across all progress-bar goals.
  • Active Goals: All goals in your account.

📈 Goal Progress Over Time

Line chart showing your actual goals created and completed, bucketed by the week they happened. Goals created before this update have no timestamp, so only new activity will appear. The chart covers the last 12 weeks and fills in organically as you use the app.

⭐ Top Goals by Progress

Your top 5 progress-bar goals. Green = 75%+, purple = 50–74%, orange = 25–49%, red = below 25%.

📋 Goal Breakdown Table

All your goals with category, type, progress bar, and status badge. Filter by category or type using the dropdowns.
